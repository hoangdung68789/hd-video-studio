For talking-head guide scripts (e.g. "cách xem sổ hồng", 2026-10-23), Dũng rejected a numbered "5 bước" legal checklist as "quá công nghiệp, không có giá trị cảm xúc". He wants: one continuous flow (liền mạch), emotional value + real practical value, trend-style "cầm sổ lên, xem đúng N chỗ", short plain sentences for người mới lần đầu mua nhà, easy like the reference video. Each point paired with the buyer's real fear/consequence (mất cọc, trả tiền cho tầng chưa hoàn công, mất phần lộ giới, ôm nợ thế chấp). Length 90–100s is fine when quality needs it (overrides a 60–90s cap in his prompt).

**Why:** viewers are first-time buyers; dry legal lists don't hold them.
**How to apply:** draft scripts as a spoken journey with consequences, not a lecture; never invent client stories — ask Dũng for a real one. Related: [[bds-video-edit-rules]], [[video-quality-feedback]]
